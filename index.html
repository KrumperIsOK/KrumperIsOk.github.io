I am currently a PhD student at UIUC, supervised by Prof. Haohan Wang. Previously, I was a Research Intern at Qwen Multilingual Team, mentored by Dr. Minghao Wu.

Before that, I obtained my master's degree from the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. Zhibo Wang, and my Bachelor's degree from Wuhan University, majoring in Computer Science and Technology.

My research focuses on effective and efficient supervision for evolving AI systems:

  • Quality and coverage: Make supervision more reliable and broadly applicable.
  • Efficient supervision: Make effective supervision affordable at scale.
  • Learning from feedback: Enable AI systems to learn and adapt more effectively from feedback.
